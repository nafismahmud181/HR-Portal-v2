# Business Configuration - User Guide

## Overview

The Business Configuration tab allows you to set up and manage your company's core operational settings including working hours, holidays, fiscal year, and remote work policies. These settings will apply across your entire organization and affect how the HRMS system calculates time, attendance, and payroll.

---

## Accessing Business Configuration

1. Log in to your HRMS account as an administrator
2. Navigate to **Admin Panel** → **Settings** → **Company Settings**
3. Click on the **Business Config** tab

---

## Working Hours & Calendar

### Setting Standard Business Hours

Define your company's regular working hours:

1. **Monday to Friday Hours**
   - Click on the **Start Time** field and select when your workday begins (e.g., 9:00 AM)
   - Click on the **End Time** field and select when your workday ends (e.g., 5:00 PM)
   - These hours will be used as the default for all employees

2. **Time Zone**
   - Select your company's time zone from the dropdown menu
   - This ensures accurate time tracking and scheduling across different locations

### Configuring Weekend Working Days

If your company operates on weekends:

1. Look for the **Weekend Working Days** section
2. Click on any day button to mark it as a working day
3. Selected days will be highlighted in orange
4. Click again to deselect
5. Common configurations:
   - **Standard**: No weekend days selected (Mon-Fri operation)
   - **Six-day week**: Select Saturday as a working day
   - **Retail/Service**: Select both Saturday and Sunday

---

## Holiday Calendar

### National Holidays

Select which national holidays your company observes:

1. Find the **National Holidays** section
2. Check the box next to each holiday your company observes
3. Available holidays include:
   - New Year's Day
   - Martin Luther King Jr. Day
   - Presidents' Day
   - Memorial Day
   - Independence Day
   - Labor Day
   - Columbus Day
   - Veterans Day
   - Thanksgiving Day
   - Christmas Day

**Note**: Checked holidays will automatically be marked as non-working days in the system.

### Company-Specific Holidays

Add holidays unique to your organization:

1. Find the **Company-Specific Holidays** section
2. Type the holiday name in the text field (e.g., "Founder's Day" or "Annual Company Retreat")
3. Click the **Add** button (or press Enter on your keyboard)
4. The holiday will appear in the list below
5. To remove a holiday, click the **Remove** button next to it

**Examples of company-specific holidays:**
- Founder's birthday
- Company anniversary
- Special cultural or religious holidays
- Team building days
- Year-end shutdown days

### Floating Holidays

Set the number of floating holidays employees can choose:

1. Find the **Floating Holidays Allocation** field
2. Enter a number between 0 and 10
3. This represents how many personal holidays employees can select throughout the year
4. Employees can typically use these for personal observances or special occasions

**Recommended allocations:**
- **0-2**: Conservative approach for small teams
- **3-5**: Standard for most organizations
- **6-10**: Generous allocation for companies prioritizing flexibility

## Fiscal Year Settings

Configure your company's fiscal year for accurate financial reporting:

### Fiscal Year Start Month

1. Click the **Fiscal Year Start Month** dropdown
2. Select the month when your fiscal year begins
3. Common options:
   - **January**: Calendar year (most common)
   - **April**: Common in some countries (e.g., UK, India)
   - **July**: Common for educational institutions
   - **October**: Common for government organizations

### Payroll Year Configuration

1. Enter the current payroll year in the **Payroll Year Configuration** field
2. This should typically match your current calendar or fiscal year
3. Example: "2024" or "2024-2025"

---

## Remote Work Policy

Configure your organization's remote work options:

### Remote Work Settings

1. **Remote work allowed**
   - Check this box if employees can work from home or any remote location
   - Unchecked = Office-only work policy

2. **Hybrid work options available**
   - Check this box if employees can split time between office and remote work
   - This allows for flexible work arrangements

### Geographic Restrictions

If you have location-based requirements for remote work:

1. Find the **Geographic Restrictions** section
2. Type a location restriction in the text field
3. Click **Add** (or press Enter)
4. The restriction will appear in the list below
5. To remove a restriction, click **Remove** next to it

**Examples of geographic restrictions:**
- "Must work within the United States"
- "Limited to Eastern Time Zone states"
- "Must be within 50 miles of headquarters"
- "Approved countries only: USA, Canada, UK"
- "Same state as office location"

**Use cases for geographic restrictions:**
- Legal compliance requirements
- Tax considerations
- Client meeting accessibility
- Security and data privacy regulations
- Time zone coordination needs

## Saving Your Changes

After configuring all settings:

1. Review your entries to ensure accuracy
2. Click the **Save Changes** button at the top right of the page
3. Wait for the success confirmation message
4. Your settings will be immediately applied across the system

**Important**: Always save your changes before navigating away from the page, or your updates will be lost.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I have different working hours for different departments?**
A: The Business Config sets company-wide defaults. For department-specific hours, use the individual employee or department settings.

**Q: What happens to employees' schedules when I change working hours?**
A: Existing schedules are preserved. New settings apply to future schedules and time calculations.

**Q: Can employees see the company holidays?**
A: Yes, all selected holidays are visible to employees in their calendars and leave management sections.

**Q: How many floating holidays should I offer?**
A: This depends on your company culture and industry standards. Most companies offer 2-5 floating holidays per year.

**Q: Can I change the fiscal year mid-year?**
A: While technically possible, it's strongly recommended to make fiscal year changes only at year-end to avoid payroll and reporting complications.

**Q: What if an employee lives in a restricted geographic area?**
A: Employees in restricted areas will not be able to request or log remote work. Discuss exceptions with HR management.
